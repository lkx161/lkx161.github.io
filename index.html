IEEE T-RL Under review · First author

IMRL-VLA: Interaction-Memory Reinforcement Learning for Vision-Restricted Contact-Rich Manipulation

“See slowly, feel fast.” A frozen VLA plans from vision, while a lightweight residual RL policy uses a 1 s command–response–force memory to correct contact at high frequency. Wrist camera only: 80.0% average success on sub-millimeter insertion, staying robust under sudden occlusion and disturbances.

Supplementary video (with narration): real-robot four-method comparison on three precision tasks, plus sudden wrist-camera occlusion and receptacle-push disturbances.

IEEE RA-L Under review

EAG-VLA: Enhancing Vision-Language-Action Models via Energy-Adaptive Guidance for Robotic Manipulation

Training-free for the backbone. The fine-tuned VLA stays frozen. A lightweight energy model scores how well actions match tactile (GelSight) or 6-axis force observations, and its gradient steers flow-matching denoising only in the late stage (t ≤ 0.3). This corrects local contact errors without disturbing the global visual plan.
